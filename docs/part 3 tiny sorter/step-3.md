# Step 3 - Setup

## Our Electronics Setup: Key Differences

The video uses an Arduino and breadboard wiring for electronics.  
**For our project:**

- You’ll use a **Raspberry Pi Pico** (no Arduino).
- All wiring is done with **Grove connectors**—just plug in, no pins or breadboard needed!
- The **servo** connects directly to your Grove shield/expansion board. No soldering or cable-matching is necessary.

> You’ll see exact wiring instructions and a reference photo in **Step 3 – Setup**.
